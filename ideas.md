# AEIM Wealth Management — Design Brainstorm

## Reference Site
Layout, section order, component structure, and visual rhythm replicate https://www.12pointconstructionllc.com/ — re-skinned for a premium wealth management brand.

## Design Direction (Client-Specified)

**Design Movement**: Premium Financial Trust — a refined blend of classical serif typography and modern minimalism, evoking established wealth management institutions.

**Core Principles**:
1. Deep navy (#0B1B2B) for dark sections/hero; clean white for content sections — alternating like the reference
2. Gold (#C9A84C) as the singular accent — underlines, stat numbers, buttons, icons
3. Generous whitespace with thin gold dividers and subtle card shadows
4. Scroll-triggered fade-up reveals on every section

**Color Philosophy**: Navy conveys institutional trust, stability, and depth — the color of established financial firms. Gold accents communicate premium quality, heritage, and discerning taste without ostentation. White sections provide clarity and breathing room for complex financial content.

**Layout Paradigm**: Full-width sections alternating between dark navy and white, matching the reference site's rhythm. Sticky transparent-to-solid nav, full-width CTA bands, two-column about teaser, numbered process steps, grid-based service cards.

**Signature Elements**:
1. Gold accent underline beneath every section headline
2. Navy/gold stat overlay on office image
3. Numbered 01-04 process steps with gold numerals

**Interaction Philosophy**: Subtle, confident motion — fade-up reveals that feel like curtains lifting, not flashy animations. Hover states deepen gold accents.

**Animation**: Framer Motion scroll-triggered fade-up reveals (y: 20 → 0, opacity 0 → 1, duration 0.5s, ease-out). Staggered children for grids.

**Typography System**:
- Headlines: Playfair Display (serif) — weights 600/700
- Body: Inter (sans-serif) — weights 400/500/600
- H1: Playfair Display 700, large
- H2: Playfair Display 600, with gold underline
- Body: Inter 400, 16-18px

**Brand Essence**: Independent fiduciary wealth management for families and individuals in DuPage County — built on transparency, not product-pushing.

**Brand Voice**: Confident, plain-spoken, advisory. No jargon. Examples:
- "Your finances, managed with your best interest at heart."
- "Not sure where to start? We'll review your finances at no cost."

**Wordmark & Logo**: "AEIM" in Playfair Display bold with a thin gold underline accent. "Wealth Management" beneath in smaller Inter tracking-wide.

**Signature Brand Color**: #C9A84C (gold)
